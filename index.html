A LETTER FROM THE DEV

Hey everyone — it's Miguel Benavides.

A small confession: this game is out here before it's on Steam. The Steam page is coming — it's in review, and review queues move at their own stately pace — but I was honestly too excited to sit quietly and wait. So I built this little corner of the internet and filled it with the things I love: a title you can destroy with your cursor, the actual DAW I composed the soundtrack in (open it, remix it, keep your mix — and as a Debussy fan, I tucked one of his pieces in there too, with some variations), and a bestiary that lovingly refuses to spoil its best secrets. I may have gotten carried away.

Here's the plan, plainly promised: the demo is free, right here, today. One zip, one double-click — no account, no Steam, no strings — and your save carries into the full game. When Early Access opens, it opens in parallel: here and on Steam, same day, same price. Buy wherever feels right — direct supports me the most, but honestly, I'm just happy you're here. A Steam wishlist meanwhile would mean the world.

This is a passion project, made with love and care, and it's a world that will keep growing. If you meet a rough edge — or just want to tell me something — write to me. I read everything. And please be a little gentle: this is my first time doing all of it by myself.

See you in the world,
— Miguel

Every Grain Of This World Is Real

Sand falls. Water finds its level. Fire spreads. Lava cools into stone. A full cellular-automata simulation, all the way down — the world doesn't play an animation of physics, it is physics.

Dig, and the world answers

Cut a channel and the ocean follows you in. Tunnel a dune and the sand chases your pickaxe grain by grain. Every block you break obeys the same rules as the mountain it came from.

A world with teeth

Meteor strikes crater the land. Swarm hunts ring the dusk sky. The Great Volcano wakes. Nights are darker, deeper, and worth surviving — tame a horse, raise crops, brew elixirs, build the base that outlasts it all.

Dig to the abyss. Sail past the clouds.

One seed, one column of world: from the galaxy sky and its crystal islands, through thirteen parallax biomes, past bioluminescent caverns, down to the deep dark where the glitter caves sing. It's all one connected map.

Seven Keepers. One Heart.

Each world scatters the Amulets of the Heart among its Keepers — seven bosses with authored arenas, and not one of them falls to sword-swinging alone. The Deep Shark's water is its armor: drain the trench, or freeze it. The Sand Leviathan swims through powder: strand it on stone. Defeat them by reshaping the world itself.
